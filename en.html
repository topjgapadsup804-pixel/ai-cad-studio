04 / Technical FAQ

Technical Questions

Can you draft accurately from a hand-drawn survey photo?

Yes. Provide at least one verified field measurement (such as entry door width or overall room span) and we calibrate the entire plan geometry with standard structural and partition wall thicknesses.

Are DWG layers and blocks unlocked for editing in AutoCAD?

Yes. Files are saved in native AutoCAD 2018 DWG format with separated structural walls, partitions, openings, furniture, MEP symbols, and dimension styles—nothing is locked or exploded.

Does the 3D spatial model require ongoing hosting subscriptions?

No. It is compiled as a single self-contained `.html` file with zero external dependencies. Open it offline on any laptop or host it free on any static web server.

Can I purchase a self-service Pro License for the web workspace?

Yes. You can draw and explore 3D free inside the live workspace, and unlock unlimited self-service DXF and A3 PDF exports anytime with a one-time $19 USD Pro License key.
